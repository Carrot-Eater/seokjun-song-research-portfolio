- Timestamp tolerance, storage precision and declared-fps-versus-runtime-Hz investigations.
- Public ecosystem declaration audits and author-fix-based evaluation ideas.

## Technical stack and methods

Python; clock mapping; episode writing; LeRobot-related formats; timestamp precision; metadata validation.

## Evidence inspected

OCLO software tree and robot_learning_data inventory. Historical evidence is described; full original experiments were not rerun for this catalog.

## Public material

## Current limits

Structural validity does not guarantee policy performance. Historical audits were not re-executed here and public dataset contents are not redistributed.
