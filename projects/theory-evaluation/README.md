**Status:** Evaluation software prototype

Compare prompt-based behavioural models while keeping inputs, budgets and evaluation units explicit.

## Work represented

- Park-inspired and theory-informed RAM, attribution, CAPS and TPB-related comparison adapters.
- Label/input separation, common budgets and quality/missingness handling.
- Counterevidence and first-impression/halo-related evaluation questions.

## Technical stack and methods

Python; prompt adapters; input hashing; paired bootstrap; Holm correction; participant/group units.

## Evidence inspected

PersonTwin theory_eval README and research inventory. Historical evidence is described; full original experiments were not rerun for this catalog.

## Public material

This card publishes an implementation/research summary. The complete source tree, runtime data and original local evidence are not part of this release.

## Current limits

Adapters are controlled prompts, not separately trained neural networks or a reproduction of proprietary Simile. Observational measures are not ground truth for general personality.

## Contribution and upstream scope

Theory and public research instruments are external sources; copyrighted instrument contents are not redistributed.
