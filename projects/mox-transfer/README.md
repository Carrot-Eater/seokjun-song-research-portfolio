# MOX cross-array and cross-device transfer

[Area index](../../areas/environmental-sensing/README.md) · [All work](../../ALL_WORK.md) · [Claim boundaries](../../EVIDENCE.md)

**Status:** Dataset experiments / manuscript preparation

**Catalog reviewed:** 12 October 2026

Test whether sensor alignment scores predict useful transfer to unseen analytes, devices or laboratories.

## Work represented

- Alignment-score versus held-out-analyte retrieval comparisons.
- Same-array/different-device transfer, zero-shot limitations and paired-calibration studies.
- Peer-sensor anchors and negative-result investigations.

## Technical stack and methods

MOX time-series processing; direct / piecewise direct standardization; fine-tuning; retrieval evaluation; controls.

## Evidence inspected

Electronic-nose REPORT, experiment/draft directories and enose_ml inventory. Historical evidence is described; full original experiments were not rerun for this catalog.

## Public material

This card publishes an implementation/research summary. The complete source tree, runtime data and original local evidence are not part of this release.

## Current limits

Dataset results are not measurements collected by a newly fabricated platform. Draft variants and negative-result notes are related studies, not separate accepted papers.

## Contribution and upstream scope

Public sensor corpora retain their original ownership; the represented work is analysis and experiment design.
